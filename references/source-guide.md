# Source guide

Starting points only. Assess each article individually. No domain is automatically reliable.

## Preferred: independent secondary sources

Scholarly books, peer-reviewed studies, specialist publications, and historical research with substantial coverage of the subject. Universities, libraries, archives, and museum catalogues count when authorship and editorial oversight are identifiable.

## Official and institutional material

Official websites, municipal records, statistical agencies, UNESCO records, legislation, and annual reports are suitable for straightforward facts within their competence (dates of founding, legal status, addresses, figures, listings). They do not establish notability and do not justify promotional claims.

## Telling kinds of material apart

- **Original reporting** versus **press release**, **sponsored content**, **opinion**, **interview**, or **copied text**. Check the byline, the date, and whether other outlets carry identical wording.
- Several sites repeating one announcement are one source, not independent corroboration.
- Interviewees' statements are attributed to them, not stated as fact.
- Opinion pieces support the author's view, attributed to the author.

## Do not use as evidence

- Wikipedia (any language), Wikidata, Wikimedia project pages, AI-generated text. Use them only to locate sources.
- Anonymous portals, unsourced blogs, social media, promotional pages.

## Search habits

- Search in Albanian, English, and any language relevant to the subject (Serbian, Turkish, German, Italian, and so on).
- Try alternative names, old and new spellings, definite and indefinite forms, transliterations, and former official names.
- Open and read each source before citing it. Record the exact passage you rely on for `|quote=`.
- Check archives (for example web.archive.org) when a page is dead, and cite the archived copy only if it was actually opened.

## When coverage is thin

Say so briefly to the user. Write a short, accurate article, or recommend not creating one, rather than inflating significance.
